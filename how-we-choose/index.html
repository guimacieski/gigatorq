Our method

How we choose

GigaTorq recommendations are research-based. We don't currently run hands-on tests. Instead, we combine verified specs, real prices and a large body of owner feedback, and we show our work on every page. If we ever test a tool ourselves, the page will say so clearly.

1. Verified specs

We collect specs from each brand's US site and from major retailers, then convert them to the same units so tools can be compared fairly. For example, "20V MAX" tools run at 18V nominal, the same as most "18V" tools, and we show both. When brands and retailers disagree, or a brand doesn't publish a number, we say so instead of guessing.

2. What owners say

We record each tool's star rating and review count at major US retailers, with the date we checked. Where reviews are publicly readable, we read them and look for patterns: problems and praise that come up again and again, not one-off stories. We summarize them in our own words, say how many reviews we read, and link to the sources. When we couldn't read enough reviews to see a pattern, the page says so. We never copy reviews or present other people's experiences as our own.

3. Price, kit and warranty

We compare what you actually get for the money: which batteries and charger come in the box, how the price compares with similar tools, and how long the warranty lasts.

4. How we use AI

GigaTorq uses AI tools to research at a scale one person couldn't manage alone: collecting spec sheets from every brand, converting them to the same units, reading owner reviews and drafting summaries.

AI doesn't decide what's true. Every number on a page comes from a brand or retailer source that we link to, and our database won't accept a spec without one. When sources disagree, the page says so instead of picking a number. An engineer with hands-on power tool experience reviews the picks before they go live.

Some images on the site are AI-generated illustrations. They're captioned "Illustration" and never presented as a photo of a specific model.

What we weigh

Every pick comes down to four things:

  • Power - torque, speed and capacity compared with tools in the same class.
  • Features - brushless motor, brakes, kickback control, lights and other useful extras.
  • Owner satisfaction - how owners rate the tool and what they report over time.
  • Value - what you get for the price, including kit contents and warranty.

Independence

Brands don't see our pages before they're published and can't pay for placement or a better score. Read our disclosure.

Keeping pages current

Prices and models change. We update pages when a new model replaces an old one or a price moves enough to change a pick, and every page shows when it was last updated.
