About GigaTorq

GigaTorq is an independent power tool comparison site for US buyers. We compare tools on verified specs, real prices and what owners report, and tell you which one to buy for the way you work.

GigaTorq is written by a mechanical assembler and engineer with years of hands-on work with power tools.

Our promise

  • We're clear about how every pick is made: research, not hands-on testing.
  • We summarize owner feedback in our own words and link to the sources.
  • We use AI to research at scale, and we say so. Every number still links to its source. How we use AI
  • Brands can't pay for placement or scores.
  • We update pages when better tools come out or prices change.

Money and independence

GigaTorq doesn't make money from its links yet. If that changes, it will never change our picks, and we'll say so on every page. Read our disclosure.
