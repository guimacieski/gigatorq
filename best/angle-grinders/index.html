Questions

How should I choose an angle grinder?

Wheel size. 4-1/2 and 5 in. grinders cover most work. 6 in. wheels cut deeper and grind faster, but the tool is bigger.

Paddle switch and no lock-on. A paddle switch you have to hold down stops the grinder the moment you let go. Many job sites require them.

Electric brake. A brake stops the wheel in a couple of seconds instead of letting it spin down, so you can set the grinder down safely sooner.

Speed. Most grinders run at 8,500 to 11,000 RPM. Variable speed helps with polishing, wire wheels and flap discs.

Weight and battery. Cordless grinders draw a lot of power. Larger batteries run longer and keep the speed up under load.

4-1/2 or 5 inch grinder?

Most 4-1/2 in. grinders also take 5 in. wheels with the right guard. 5 in. wheels cut a little deeper and last longer.

Paddle switch or slide switch?

A paddle switch with no lock-on is safer because the tool stops when you let go. Slide switches can lock on.

Can an angle grinder cut tile and stone?

Yes, with a diamond wheel. Use water or dust control, since stone dust is harmful to breathe.

What safety gear do I need?

Eye protection and a face shield, gloves, hearing protection, and always keep the guard on.
