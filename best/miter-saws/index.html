DeWalt's DWS780 is a 12 in. double-bevel sliding saw that crosscuts 2x16 lumber at 90 degrees and 2x12 at 45. It cuts 7-1/2 in. crown nested and 6-3/4 in. base vertically, has 10 positive stops and a 15-amp motor. It weighs 56 lb.

Questions

How should I choose a miter saw?

Blade size. 10 in. saws are lighter and blades are cheaper. 12 in. saws cut wider and thicker stock.

Sliding or non-sliding. Sliding rails let the blade travel forward, so you can cut boards 12 to 16 in. wide. Non-sliding saws are smaller, lighter and cheaper.

Single or double bevel. A double-bevel saw tilts both ways, so you don't have to flip long trim pieces for opposite bevels.

Capacity. Check crosscut capacity at 90 and 45 degrees, plus crown (nested) and base (vertical) capacity if you install trim.

Corded or cordless. Corded saws are cheaper and run all day. Cordless saws are great for trim work in houses without power yet.

10 or 12 inch miter saw?

A 10 in. saw handles most trim and DIY work. Choose a 12 in. saw for wide boards, thick stock or big crown molding.

Do I need a sliding miter saw?

Only if you cut boards wider than about 6 to 8 inches. Otherwise a non-sliding saw is smaller and lighter.

What are positive stops?

Detents at common angles like 0, 22.5 and 45 degrees, so you can set accurate angles quickly.

Can a miter saw cut metal?

Not with a wood blade. Some saws can take special non-ferrous blades for aluminum. Check the manual first.
